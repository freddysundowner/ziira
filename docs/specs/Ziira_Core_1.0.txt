OVERVIEW
Core and Intelligence
Ziira Core runs the business. Ziira Intelligence checks it every day and tells owners what
needs attention, on screen and on WhatsApp.
Ziira Core
Our ERP. Runs sales, stock, purchasing,
payments, accounting, HR and payroll for every
branch, and lets people act.
Ziira Intelligence
Reads the business's data, matches records
against money and stock, explains what changed,
and recommends what to do.
Intelligence works in two ways. It is built into Ziira Core, where it has full, live access. It also runs on
its own on top of systems businesses already use: Odoo, Zoho, QuickBooks, ERPNext, POS systems,
M-Pesa and bank statements. We lead in the market with Intelligence, because it needs no switching.
Businesses that trust it, or have no system yet, move to Ziira Core.
Who Ziira Core 1.0 is for
Retail and distribution businesses where staff handle money and stock while the owner is away:
Multi-branch shops: hardware, agrovets, pharmacies, wines and spirits, mini-marts
Distributors and wholesalers selling on credit
Single-outlet businesses with an absent owner or high cash volume
Version 1.0 is designed for this one segment. Service and project businesses follow in 1.x.

02 · COMMAND CENTER
The Command Center
The Command Center is a Core module; its intelligence comes from Ziira Intelligence. It is
the first screen for owners and managers, and it also reaches them on WhatsApp.
Part What it shows or does
Business pulse Sales, cash received, stock, receivables, payables and expenses, per branch
What needs attention Unmatched sales, stock losses, overdue customers, unusual voids or discounts,
pending approvals
Insights What changed and why, across sales, stock, purchasing and cash
Recommendations What to consider doing next, with the evidence behind it
Ask Ziira Questions in English or Swahili, on screen or on WhatsApp
Source records From any finding to the records behind it
On WhatsApp Daily brief, urgent alerts, answers and one-reply approvals
Principle: Core supplies the records; Intelligence checks and interprets them; the Command
Center brings what matters to the owner, wherever they are; Core lets them act.
What Intelligence checks in 1.0
Money: sales against M-Pesa, cash and bank, per branch and cashier
Stock: movements that sales do not explain; stockouts; expiry
Controls: voids, refunds, discounts, price overrides and stock adjustments above normal; one
person creating and approving
Credit: late payers; credit given to overdue customers
Suppliers: price rises not passed on; duplicate bills
Cash, books and tax: weeks when payments exceed expected cash; unusual journal entries;
invoices missing from eTIMS
People and payroll: sales by staff absent that day; overtime trends; new names, pay changes or
bank changes before payroll is approved

03 · DATA DESIGN
Intelligence-ready by design
Ziira Core is not an ERP with intelligence added later. Its data model makes daily
checking possible from the first transaction.
Every important record has a unique identifier.
Every transaction carries a timestamp, branch, source, status and user.
Every payment carries its method and external reference, such as the M-Pesa code.
Voids, refunds, discounts, price overrides and stock adjustments require a reason code.
Who created and who approved are recorded separately.
Nothing is hard-deleted; cancelled records stay visible.
Customers, suppliers, products, payments, orders and stock movements are linked.
Stock movements connect to the sale, purchase, transfer or adjustment that caused them.
History is retained, so Intelligence understands what is normal and what changed.
The same structured data is exposed through APIs, so Intelligence works the same way in Core and
on other systems.
Accounting and payroll
Version 1.0 includes a general ledger. Every sale, purchase, payment, expense and stock movement
posts to the books automatically, so the business gets a profit and loss statement, balance sheet and
VAT figures without anyone entering data twice. Accountants who prefer their own software can still
receive an export.
Payroll posts to the ledger too, and its statutory deductions (PAYE, SHIF, NSSF and the Housing Levy)
are kept as rate tables that can be updated when the law changes, without changing code.
Because errors here are costly, the ledger and payroll are tested heavily before launch, checked by an
accountant against official calculations, and the ledger is checked daily against the Payments and
Reconciliation module.
