OVERVIEW
What Ziira Intelligence is
Ziira Intelligence is the analysis and alerting layer of Ziira. It connects to a business's
systems, checks the data every day, and delivers findings and recommended actions to
owners and managers on WhatsApp and in the Command Center.
What it does
Function Description
Detect Finds losses, errors and risks: money not received, stock not accounted for, unusual
entries, late payers, cost increases, missed deadlines.
Explain States the cause, linking areas of the business, and shows the records behind every
finding.
Recommend and
act
Lists options ranked by importance, and sends follow-ups, reminders and approval
requests within permissions the business sets.
Deployment
Mode Description
Inside Ziira Core Built into the ERP and its Command Center. Live access to all 12 modules; can prepare
actions in Core for approval.
Standalone Runs on top of existing systems: Odoo, Zoho, QuickBooks, ERPNext, SAP Business One,
POS systems, M-Pesa and bank statements. The business keeps its current system. This
is the market entry point; businesses can later move to Ziira Core.

Target customers
Businesses where staff handle money or stock without direct owner supervision, and where losses cost
more than the subscription: multi-branch retailers, distributors, absent-owner outlets, and high-volume cash businesses.

Yes, for most systems. The data belongs to the business, and most modern systems let
the business give an approved app read access. Some add conditions or costs.

How it works
Ziira runs a four-step cycle every day.

ERP, POS, M-Pesa,
bank, staff reports
2. Check
Records vs money,
normal vs unusual
3. Tell
Briefs, alerts and

4. Act
Owner replies,
Ziira follows up
Repeated every day, learning what is normal for the business
1. Connect: collect the latest data from every connected source.
2. Check: compare records (what staff entered: sales, stock moves, expenses, closing reports) with
evidence (what is hard to change: M-Pesa, bank, supplier invoices, stock counts), and compare
today with what is normal.
3. Tell: send a short message, most important items first, with the records behind each finding.
4. Act: on the owner's reply, Ziira asks staff to explain, sends reminders, or prepares approvals.
Numbers come from the records, not AI guesses. Totals and gaps are calculated directly from
the data. AI explains, ranks and answers questions in English or Swahili.

Control checks
An ERP accepts any valid entry. Ziira checks the entries themselves, every day:
Manual stock adjustments
Price and discount overrides
Voided sales, refunds and credit notes
Back-dated or late entries
Changes at unusual times
One person creating and approving
Changed supplier bank details
Possible duplicate bills or payments
The most valuable findings combine areas, such as a cash shortfall explained by payroll timing, a
supplier payment and late customer payments.
Flags are questions, not accusations. Most have a good explanation. Ziira shows the evidence
and records each answer, which protects honest staff as well as the business.

04 · PEOPLE AND ACTIONS
Who receives what, and what they can do
In a small business the owner may be the only user. In a larger one, each role receives
what is relevant, within permissions the owner sets.
Role Receives
Owner or director Daily or weekly brief across the business, urgent alerts, answers to any question
Finance manager Cash forecast, reconciliation gaps, receivables, payables, control flags, tax deadlines
Branch manager Their branch only: sales against money, stock issues, staff flags
Accountant or auditor Reconciled figures and open flags with explanations, with the owner's permission

05 · TRUST AND START
Trust, limits and rollout
How Ziira protects trust
Read-only by default, through a dedicated key the business can withdraw.
Role-based visibility: a branch manager does not see payroll.
Evidence with every finding, linked to the records behind it.
Data protection: compliance with Kenya's Data Protection Act, with clear consent and clarity on
where data is stored and which AI services process it.
Limits
It cannot see what is never recorded anywhere.
It shows gaps; it does not prove wrongdoing.
Some alerts will be wrong, especially in the first weeks, until it learns the business.
On other systems, what it can read and do depends on their access terms.
It does not replace an accountant, auditor or tax adviser.
Onboarding
When What happens
Day 1 Connect the system, M-Pesa and bank; confirm the system, version and plan
Days 2–5 Map branches, staff, accounts and payment methods; read past months so Ziira learns
what is normal
Week 2 First briefs, plus a one-off review of past records showing what Ziira would have caught
Weeks 3–4 Adjust alerts with the owner; add managers who should receive briefs
Validation
Before full launch, Ziira runs a 30-day pilot with 5 to 6 businesses, some on an ERP and some without.
Success criteria: most pilot businesses pay to continue, and Ziira identifies problems they were not
aware of.
