# Pack: consultant, agency, accountant or lawyer (`pack_professional`)

An industry pack (data only, `docs/industry-packs.md`) for a practice that bills time. Switch it on in Add-ons or at sign-up
(Services edition). Everything it adds can be edited; nothing is overwritten and what you delete is not brought back.

## What it sets up
* **Service items** (no prices): consulting, accounting, tax, audit, legal, creative, retainer, disbursements. Settings point at
  "Consulting and advisory" (time) and "Disbursements and expenses" (re-billed costs).
* **Settings** suggested while you have not saved your own: 5-minute steps, partner-free approval, reminders when a day has under
  6 hours, reminders to approvers after 2 and 5 days.
* **Engagement templates** (9): monthly bookkeeping, annual accounts and tax return (3 milestones), audit (capped), payroll service,
  legal matter (hourly and capped), consulting project (fixed fee), agency retainer, campaign project. Each has a checklist; some repeat.
* **Example rates** by title (Partner ... Paralegal): marked **Example**, they price nothing until confirmed on the Rates page.
* **Activities** (25) for accountants, auditors, agencies and lawyers, and three not-billed ones.
* **Standard invoice wording** in English and Swahili (payment thanks, schedule attached, fixed-fee milestone, retainer, disbursements, fee cap).
* **Custom fields**: client - kind of work, year-end, main contact, tax number; project - matter number, other side, client's reference.
* **Intelligence limits** for the unbilled, approval, unsent-week, retainer and realisation checks (only for checks you never tuned).
* **Sample practice** (on request, removable): four clients, projects of four fee types, a week of your time, two expenses, deadlines.

## First run (Time and billing > Overview shows these and ticks them itself)
1. Choose what invoices are made under (Settings). 2. Confirm your rates (Rates). 3. Give people titles (Rates > People).
4. Open a client project from a template. 5. Log an hour. 6. Approve a week and send the first invoice.

## What it does not do
Trust or client money; tax or statutory advice (no due dates are invented: the template dates are examples you set); prices
(the example rates are placeholders in your own currency).
