# International defaults (country pack)

The pack a business gets at sign-up when no country pack names its country (or when the operator has installed no packs).
It adds three tax codes that charge nothing (`NONE`, `ZERO`, `EXEMPT`); the business adds the rate its country charges in
Products > Tax codes. It supplies no chart of accounts (the Finance module's general chart already fits any country) and no
payroll tables (the business enters the rules its country's law requires: People > Statutory rules).

See `docs/localisation.md` and `docs/writing-a-plugin.md` ("Country packs").
